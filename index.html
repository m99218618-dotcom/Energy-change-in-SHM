Physics behind the energy exchange

x(t) = A cos(ωt)
v(t) = −Aω sin(ωt)
U = ½kx² = ½kA² cos²(ωt)
K = ½mv² = ½kA² sin²(ωt)
E = K + U = ½kA²
ω = √(k/m)

At x = ±A, U is maximum and K = 0. At equilibrium x = 0, K is maximum and U = 0. At all intermediate positions, the two forms of energy exchange while their sum remains unchanged.

Student investigation: double the amplitude without changing the spring constant. What happens to total mechanical energy? Total energy becomes four times larger because E ∝ A².
